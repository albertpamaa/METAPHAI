👋 About us

A personal project to
understand your money

Metaphai began with a simple question: why does no one in Spain teach us the basics about taxes, payslips or personal finance?

Who is behind it?

🔬
Albert Parra
PhD in Optical Engineering · Biomedical Researcher

I am a scientist by training, with a PhD in optical engineering, and a biomedical researcher. My day-to-day work takes place among laboratories and data, not tax spreadsheets.

But, like anyone who works and pays taxes in Spain, there came a time when I needed to understand what was deducted from my payslip, what I could receive if I became unemployed, or how much tax I would have to pay on an inheritance. I discovered that finding this information clearly, up to date and free of charge was surprisingly difficult.

So I built it myself.

“In Spain we learn mathematics, history and literature, but nobody explains how personal income tax works, what a taxable base is or what a mortgage really costs us. That is what I am trying to change with Metaphai.”

What is Metaphai?

Metaphai is a collection of free financial calculators focused on Spain. The tools provide indicative estimates and explain their main assumptions; regulations change and results should be checked before making decisions.

The project started as a personal experiment to learn web development and taxation at the same time. It has gradually grown into something I hope will be useful to anyone who wants to understand their financial situation better.

In the long term, the aim is for the website to support itself financially, mainly through non-intrusive advertising and collaborations with relevant financial services, so that it can continue to be maintained and improved.

Our principles

🆓
Always free
All calculators are and will remain free. No registration and no subscriptions.
🔒
Local calculations
The data entered in calculators are processed in your browser and the calculation logic does not send them to the server.
📅
Independent project
Metaphai is a personal project and does not represent the Spanish Tax Agency, Social Security or any other public body.
🎯
Indicative and honest
We are clear: the calculators are estimates, not substitutes for a tax adviser.

An important notice

Metaphai is a guidance tool, not a tax or legal advisory service. Results are estimates based on current general rules and may not reflect particular or complex tax situations.

For important decisions such as a tax return, inheritance or home purchase, we always recommend consulting a certified tax manager or adviser.
