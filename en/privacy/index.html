Metaphai.com does not require registration and does not directly request personal data through user accounts or forms.

If you consent, Google Analytics and Google AdSense may process cookies, online identifiers and technical browsing data, including:

Browsing data are used exclusively for the following purposes:

In territories where applicable, consent for Google services is managed through Google’s consent management platform (CMP). The message allows users to consent, not consent or manage purposes, and communicates those choices through Google Consent Mode. More details are available in our Cookie Policy.

We use Google Analytics to measure website use and produce aggregated statistics. Google may process online identifiers and technical browsing data. Analytics storage depends on the choice communicated by the CMP and the applicable Consent Mode configuration.

4.2 Google AdSense cookies

We use Google AdSense to display advertising. Advertising storage and personalisation depend on the choice communicated by the CMP and the applicable Consent Mode configuration. You can manage ad personalisation in Google Ads Settings.

4.3 How to disable cookies

You can review or change your choices through the privacy options provided by Google’s consent message when available. You can also configure your browser to delete or block cookies; some website functions may be affected.

This website participates in the Google AdSense programme. As a third-party provider, Google uses cookies to serve ads on this website. Google uses the DART cookie to serve ads to users based on their visit to this and other websites.

Users can opt out of the DART cookie by visiting Google’s Privacy Policy.

Where processing through analytics or advertising storage requires consent, the choice is collected and communicated through Google’s CMP and Consent Mode. The specific legal basis may depend on the service, purpose, configuration and applicable law.
