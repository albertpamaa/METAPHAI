Indicator

Demography

  • Total population — Number of residents at mid-year under the de facto definition.
  • Life expectancy at birth — Years a newborn would live if the year’s mortality patterns remained constant.
  • Total fertility rate — Hypothetical births per woman under the age-specific fertility rates observed in the year.
  • Population growth — Population growth · % per year.
  • Infant mortality — Infant mortality · per 1,000 live births.
  • Urban population — Urban population · % of population.
  • Population ages 65 and above — Population ages 65 and above · % of population.
  • Population density — Population density · people per km².

Economy

  • GDP per capita, PPP — Gross domestic product per person converted using purchasing power parities.
  • Annual GDP growth — Annual change in GDP at constant prices.
  • Consumer price inflation — Consumer price inflation · % per year.

Employment

  • Total unemployment — Share of the labour force without work, available for work and seeking employment.
  • Youth unemployment — Youth unemployment · % of labor force ages 15–24.
  • Labor force participation — Labor force participation · % of population ages 15+.

Technology and connectivity

  • Individuals using the Internet — Share of people who used the Internet during the previous three months.
  • Fixed broadband subscriptions — Fixed broadband subscriptions · per 100 people.

Energy and environment

  • Renewable energy consumption — Renewables as a share of total final energy consumption.
  • CO₂ emissions per capita — CO₂ emissions per capita · t CO₂e per person.
  • Access to electricity — Access to electricity · % of population.
  • Forest area — Forest area · % of land area.

Society

  • Current health expenditure — Current health expenditure · % of GDP.
  • Maternal mortality — Maternal mortality · per 100,000 live births.

Limitations

  • Total population: Figures are mid-year population estimates. Changes are calculated only when observations exist at both endpoints.
  • Life expectancy at birth: A period measure based on current mortality rates, not a prediction for an individual.
  • Total fertility rate: The sum of period age-specific fertility rates; it is not the completed fertility of a woman or real cohort.
  • GDP per capita, PPP: Because values are in current international dollars, changes over time are not real growth and do not represent personal disposable income.
  • Annual GDP growth: Change from the previous year in a constant-price series; historical differences are shown in percentage points.
  • Total unemployment: ILO modelled estimate. Historical differences are expressed in percentage points.
  • Individuals using the Internet: Availability and estimation methods vary across countries and years; changes are expressed in percentage points.
  • Renewable energy consumption: This measures final energy consumption, not electricity generation alone. Changes are expressed in percentage points.
  • Population growth: WDI observations are shown without interpolation by MetaphAI; reference years and coverage can differ across countries.
