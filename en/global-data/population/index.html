Population by country

Compare population, rankings and historical trends across countries.

What it measures

Total population estimates the number of people residing in a country or territory at mid-year. It uses a de facto definition, counting residents regardless of citizenship or legal status. The measure supports comparisons of population size and long-term growth or decline, but it does not describe geographic distribution, age structure or seasonal movement. Values are people; the interface may abbreviate chart and card values while detailed tables and CSV retain the received number.

How to interpret it

A higher value means a larger estimated resident population, not greater density, wellbeing or growth. Trends should be assessed by comparing observations for the same country.

Limitations

  • The series combines censuses, registers, surveys and estimates; demographic revisions can alter earlier years.
  • Small territories and highly mobile populations may carry greater uncertainty.
  • A mid-year estimate does not represent the population on a specific date.
